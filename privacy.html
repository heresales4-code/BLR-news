Privacy Policy

Last updated: [fill in date when you publish this]

This page explains what information BLR. ("the app", "we") collects when you use it, how it's used, and the choices you have. This app aggregates publicly available news headlines from third-party publishers — we don't require you to create an account to use it.

Information stored in your browser

  • Saved / bookmarked stories are stored locally in your browser (via localStorage) and are never sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data will remove them.
  • Search terms you type are used only to filter the current page and are not logged or stored anywhere.

Push notifications

If you choose to enable notifications, your browser generates a push subscription (an endpoint identifier, not tied to your name or email) which we store solely to deliver news alerts to you. You can disable notifications at any time from within the app, which removes this subscription from our records.

Server logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically logs basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and troubleshooting purposes. We don't use this to identify individual visitors.

Advertising and cookies

This site may display ads served by third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdSense). These networks may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and opt out of personalized advertising, at Google's Partner Sites policy and Google Ad Settings.

Third-party news content

Headlines and summaries shown in this app are sourced from publicly published RSS feeds of third-party news publishers, with links back to the original articles. Clicking through takes you to the publisher's own website, which has its own separate privacy policy that we don't control.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the app evolves. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
